Penetration Testing Foundations

Lesson 1: Methodology

Testing process and phases

Penetration testing is a structured security assessment process rather than a random set of attacks.

Focus points

  • Penetration testing is part of the broader discipline of ethical hacking.
  • The penetration testing life cycle mirrors ethical hacking phases but emphasizes reporting at the end.
  • Frameworks such as OWASP, OSSTMM, and NIST SP 800-115 help standardize assessment practices.

Summary

Strong methodology keeps testing defensible, repeatable, and useful to stakeholders.

Exam angle

Remember that reporting is the major distinction between the penetration-testing life cycle and the basic hacking methodology.

Lesson 2: Scope Types

Box testing and team roles

Different test types simulate different threat perspectives and information levels.

Focus points

  • Black-box testing simulates an outside attacker with little or no prior information.
  • White-box testing provides full internal knowledge for deep assessment coverage.
  • Gray-box testing simulates an insider or partially informed attacker and usually reduces reconnaissance effort.

Summary

Red teams attack, blue teams defend, and purple teams help connect lessons across both.

Exam angle

If the scenario says the tester has partial knowledge, think gray box.

Module focus

Methodologies, red/blue team roles, scope types, and reporting.

Why it matters

Understand how a penetration test is planned, executed, and documented.
