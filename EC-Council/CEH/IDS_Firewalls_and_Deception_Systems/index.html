Module XI

IDS, Firewalls & Deception Systems

This module covers intrusion detection systems, firewall behavior and evasion, and honeypots used for controlled observation or decoy defense.

Take Module Quiz
Study content
Lesson 1: Detection and Filtering

Seeing and shaping traffic

IDS and firewalls help defenders detect suspicious behavior and restrict network exposure.

Focus points

  • IDS platforms identify patterns, policy violations, and notable traffic behaviors.
  • Firewalls enforce boundary rules and segmentation choices.
  • Attackers may attempt evasion or detection avoidance to slip past these controls.

Summary

Detection and enforcement controls work best when visibility and policy are both strong.

Exam angle

If the question asks which device monitors for suspicious traffic patterns, IDS is often the right direction.

Lesson 2: Honeypots

Deception as defense

Honeypots act as controlled targets that help defenders observe malicious behavior or divert attackers.

Focus points

  • They can reveal probing patterns, malware behavior, and attacker tradecraft.
  • A honeypot should be isolated and monitored carefully so it does not become a real pivot point.
  • Deception is useful for intelligence gathering as well as alerting.

Summary

A good honeypot is valuable because normal users should not be interacting with it at all.

Exam angle

If traffic hits a decoy system that should have no business use, it is probably worth investigating.

Module focus

Detection, evasion, perimeter control, and honeypot-based observation.

Why it matters

Modern defenses watch, filter, and sometimes intentionally lure attackers.
