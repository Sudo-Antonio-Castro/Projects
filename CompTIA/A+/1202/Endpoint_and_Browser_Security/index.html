Account Security

Strong identity practices reduce the success of common attacks.

Core Ideas

  • Use strong unique passwords, MFA, and lockout thresholds.
  • Disable stale accounts and avoid shared credentials where possible.
  • Password resets should follow identity verification policy.

Support Focus

  • Repeated failed sign-ins can signal confusion, sync issues, or attack activity.
  • Single sign-on can improve usability but still depends on strong primary account security.
  • Identity hygiene matters on every endpoint.
Summary

Account security is both a policy issue and a daily support issue.

A+ Exam Tip

Reused passwords turn one compromise into many.

Workstation & Browser Security

A hardened endpoint is patched, protected, and limited to the access it needs.

Core Ideas

  • Keep OS and apps patched and verify endpoint protection status.
  • Enable host-based firewall protections and restrict unnecessary admin rights.
  • Unexpected redirects and pop-ups often point to malicious extensions or adware.

Support Focus

  • Security problems often first appear as slowness or browser oddities.
  • Review browser extensions, settings, and update status during triage.
  • Do not teach users to ignore warnings just to keep moving.
Summary

Endpoint and browser security failures often show up as usability complaints.

A+ Exam Tip

Unexpected toolbars and redirects should make you think browser abuse or adware.

Troubleshoot Workstation Security

Security troubleshooting is controlled, documented, and policy-driven.

Core Ideas

  • Follow approved malware response procedures rather than improvising.
  • Containment and evidence preservation may matter depending on policy.
  • Disconnecting from the network may be necessary, but follow process.

Support Focus

  • After cleanup, validate patches, account integrity, browser settings, and data exposure risk.
  • Escalate suspected breach conditions quickly.
  • Document tools used and final status in the ticket.
Summary

A compromised endpoint requires process, not guesswork.

A+ Exam Tip

Containment, evidence, and approved tools are common exam themes.
