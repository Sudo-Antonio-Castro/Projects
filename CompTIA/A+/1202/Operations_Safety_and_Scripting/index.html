Change & Inventory Management

Operational maturity depends on knowing what assets exist and how changes are controlled.

Core Ideas

  • Change requests document what is changing, why it matters, and how risk will be handled.
  • Approvals, scheduling, validation, and rollback planning reduce downtime risk.
  • Asset inventories track devices, owners, locations, serials, and warranty details.

Support Focus

  • Documentation must be updated when systems are added, moved, or retired.
  • Inventory accuracy supports budgeting, auditing, and incident response.
  • Change management is tied directly to communication and accountability.
Summary

If you cannot describe the environment accurately, you cannot support it well.

A+ Exam Tip

Risk analysis, approvals, and back-out planning are common exam themes.

Safety, Environmental Procedures & Scripting

Support work must protect people and systems, and scripts help make routine work consistent.

Core Ideas

  • Use ESD precautions, correct PPE, safe lifting, and approved disposal methods for toner, batteries, and electronics.
  • Temperature, humidity, airflow, dust, and power quality affect system reliability.
  • Safety documentation and signage are part of readiness, not just compliance.

Support Focus

  • Scripts automate repeatable tasks and reduce manual mistakes.
  • Common scripting ideas include variables, comments, loops, and conditionals.
  • Test scripts before production use and document what they do.
Summary

Safe support and repeatable automation are both operational best practices.

A+ Exam Tip

Comments explain code; scripts are best used for repeatable, well-understood tasks.
