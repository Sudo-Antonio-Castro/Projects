## Learning Resources

- **Study modules** organize concepts into focused lessons and topic areas.
- **Module quizzes** provide a review checkpoint after studying a subject.
- **Midterms and final practice exams** bring multiple topics together for cumulative review.

## Suggested Study Flow

Choose a track, work through its modules, and complete the corresponding quizzes. Revisit topics that need more practice before moving to the midterm or final assessment. Use the [CLI Labs](../CLIs/) to reinforce command-line concepts with guided exercises.

For local viewing or editing, keep each track's supporting files and the shared [assets](../assets/) in their original relative locations.

## Study Note

Use these resources as supplemental learning material alongside the published objectives for your target exam. Practice results are not official certification scores and do not guarantee an exam outcome.
